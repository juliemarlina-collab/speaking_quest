How to Play & Controls

⌨️ Desktop & Camera Controls

  • W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys: Run & steer hero
  • Spacebar: Jump (bounce high on red mushrooms!)
  • Drag Mouse or C Key: Orbit 360° / Switch Selfie Camera
  • E Key / 🎙️ TALK: Speak with a Guardian

🪞 See Yourself & Audio Features

  • 🎥 Cam Button: Switch between Close, Selfie Front, Action & Top views
  • 🪞 Mirror: Show or hide your Hero Mirror card
  • 🎙️ Speaking: Hear the task, choose your help, press Start and speak. Press Stop, fix the words if needed, then tick your checklist.
  • ⌨️ No microphone? Type instead (marked as a typed answer).

🏆 Scoring & Star Rating Rules

⭐ Meaningful attempt: 1 star
⭐ Reason / detail: +1 star
⭐ Target structure: +1 star (100 pts per star)
💎 Crystal Coin: +10 pts
🎁 Hidden Chest: +100 pts
🌀 Level Clear: +300 pts

🔒 Your voice & privacy

Speech-to-text uses your browser's speech service, which may send audio to the browser company (for example Google in Chrome). Recordings stay on this device for playback only and are never uploaded or saved. Progress and (optionally) transcripts are saved on this device only; delete them any time in Settings. The game never scores accent or pronunciation and does not give MUET bands.

3D scenery: Kenney Nature Kit (CC0). 3D engine: Three.js.
